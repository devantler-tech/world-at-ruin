# Complete ordinary client scene proof

> 🤖 Generated by the Agentic Engineer

The ordinary client path has three parts. The dedicated job retains the six
non-skippable product-law scenes and its existing workflow, historical-state,
native interaction, boot and parallel-save controls. Three isolated scene
workers run the remaining discovered scenes. A short reducer proves their
complete union before the stable required check can succeed. The independent
trusted sandbox evaluation is unchanged.

## Selection and isolation

The planner discovers tracked, regular, immediately contained
`client/tests/*_test.tscn` files at the tested integration commit. Missing,
symlinked, dirty or untracked scene inputs fail closed. The optional tracked
`ci-skip.txt` retains the serial loop's policy: blank and comment lines are
ignored; a scene is skipped only by its exact basename on a complete line.
Leading/trailing spaces do not silently become a matching entry.

The six dedicated scenes are never moved into the skippable set:
`save_fixture_guard_test`, `creature_factory_test`, `save_vault_guard_test`,
`boot_recovery_guard_test`, `vault_restore_boot_test` and `ability_registry_test`.
All six must exist and retain their separate required steps.

The remaining sorted scene names are distributed round-robin over fixed workers
0, 1 and 2. Every worker must be nonempty. Each gets a separate hosted runner,
pinned checksum-verified engine, editor import and private save directory.
Scenes still execute through `run-client-test.sh`; the existing process-group
supervisor owns the worker step and its nested engines. No assertion, scene
deadline or verdict rule is replaced by the partition.

## Completion, not a count-only claim

Each worker journals a scene only after its real runner succeeds. It refuses a
reused output directory. After every assigned scene completes, it deletes and
checks the absence of its private save state. Only then can the planner seal a
receipt for that exact ordered shard.

The receipt binds the full integration SHA, workflow run, attempt, shard and
whole-plan digest, including every scene's content and the skip input. Its
immutable artifact name carries the same identities. The reducer downloads
only this run's matching artifacts, retaining separate artifact directories,
and independently reconstructs all three expected receipts from its checkout.
It requires exactly three real directories, each containing only its regular
receipt. Canonical byte equality rejects omissions, duplicates, wrong identity,
partial output, extra JSON fields and duplicate JSON keys without interpreting
artifact content as executable code.

The stable gate separately requires successful dedicated, sandbox and scene
proof jobs, cleaned completion, a matching integration, and the scene reducer's
matching run and attempt. A skipped or failed worker is never accepted as a
successful matrix. A previous attempt's completed proof cannot satisfy a new
aggregate attempt.

Cloth selection is event-aware. On a pull request, only selected-and-successful
capture or explicitly unselected-and-skipped capture is valid. On a merge queue
entry, the pull-request-only detector and capture are skipped, so an empty
selection with a skipped capture is also valid. Empty selection on a pull
request, unknown events and contradictory results remain failures.

**Rerun the complete CI workflow after a failure.** A failed-jobs-only rerun
mixes attempts and deliberately cannot satisfy this proof. Do not rename old
artifacts, forge output fields or relax identity checks to make a retry green.
