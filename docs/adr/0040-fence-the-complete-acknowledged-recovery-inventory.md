# Fence the complete acknowledged recovery inventory

Status: accepted for a default-off experiment (#1325); production adoption remains gated by #1315.

## Context

ADR 0039 excludes competing recoverers through one generation-wide private Nakama
create. That owner ACK alone does not fence a delayed allocation PUT. The source
may die with several issued targets, and a recoverer may die after any barrier.
Neither a visible owner row nor a later barrier nonce proves an ACK was received.

## Decision

The originating live owner's opaque reservation carries one shared consume-once
attempt. Consumption precedes Kubernetes I/O and survives copied reservations and
independently constructed clients. Diagnostic acceptance and persisted decoding
cannot reconstruct this handle. Recovery takes no caller-supplied target list.

A barrier-only client consumes the original complete pinned handoff: generation,
incarnation, root and handoff versions, namespace/Fleet, membership, owner ID and
owner ACK version, and every actor/attempt/name/UID/original resourceVersion. It
never calls Prepare, emits an allocation PUT, refreshes an allocation version,
restores an old receipt, retries an uncertain attempt, or releases quarantine.

All targets share one bounded deadline. A fresh GET must identify the original
target without deletion or a preexisting barrier. Only original-version Ready
with no attempt, or Allocated with the original attempt and a different version,
has a known outcome. The latter records allocation before the barrier. Changed
Ready history, missing/replaced targets and foreign attempts remain unknown.

The journal does not contain original Spec/Status bytes. Recovery freezes the
fresh accepted GET's complete Spec and Status, adds only a fresh per-target
metadata nonce, and performs a CAS at that observed version. Its exact ACK must
preserve identity, outcome, attempt, Spec and Status and supply a version distinct
from both observed and original versions. Exact readback must match that ACK and
nonce. The existing transport forbids redirects, client-go retries and automatic
PUT replay. Any lost/partial/canceled response consumes the whole attempt.

Only all-target success with a live context produces an opaque process-local
complete result. Its detached diagnostics include the unchanged full handoff
and every barrier observation; they cannot construct new authority. Partial
success, a visible nonce, and same-ID or different-ID restart cannot restore it.
This slice publishes no durable complete proof. That independent publication,
authenticated pin custody, reader rollout, retained artifacts and production
activation remain separate parent gates.

## Evidence and retirement

Unit tests exercise consumption races, complete mixed outcomes, uncertain ACKs,
readback mismatches, changed history and transport refusal. The packaged native
trial registers two actual targets, kills and joins the source, then runs fresh
Nakama recoverers against the owned Kubernetes/etcd control plane. Controls show
unfenced original writes succeed, successful barriers reject them, and failures
never export a complete result. A final crash occurs after local complete-proof
acceptance but before export to the supervisor; no durable publication is claimed.

The native-only probe is default off and belongs to retirement issue #1316.
Normal artifacts and all existing immutable schema readers remain unchanged.
