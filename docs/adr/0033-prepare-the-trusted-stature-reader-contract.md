# 0033. Prepare the trusted stature reader contract before expansion

Status: Accepted

## Context

The protected regression workflow selects the World at Ruin base supplied by GitHub. It replaces
candidate tests, history and engine settings with that reviewed snapshot. A candidate cannot invent
its own recipe fixture or a permissive stage test and call the result independent validation.
The current reader accepts recipe v4 and capability 7. Issue #593 needs the next reader to understand
independent upper/lower leg lengths while existing writers remain unchanged.

## Decision

Land preparation before reader activation. Keep recipe reads/writes at v4 and capability reads/writes
at capability 7 in the preparation tree. Separate the recipe origination ceiling from its read ceiling.

The reviewed base owns one exact planned v5 fixture. The protected controller accepts unchanged
recipe history or its exact next v5 append. For the latter, candidate fixture bytes must match the
reviewed plan exactly; the evaluation tree receives the trusted copy. Once v5 is in the reviewed
history, only that unchanged history and its trusted golden remain accepted. Missing inputs,
symlinks, changed history, changed future fixture and recipe v6 are refused before import.
The existing capability declaration boundary remains unchanged; capability 8 writes are refused.

The stage policy recognizes only the retained mastery reader/writer combinations and the planned
recipe v5/capability 8 reader with recipe v4/capability 7 writer. The base-owned stature test
requires independent segment changes, actual skinned height, unchanged historical fingerprints,
knee/ankle garment clearance and exact preservation of pre-existing expanded state. It also
requires both creator and store to refuse originating or editing reader-only leg values.

## Consequences

Preparation is reviewable and passes against today's unchanged characters. It gives the later
expansion a trusted oracle without activating that expansion. The v5 reader still needs its own
merged delivery, immutable published read metadata, retained whole-app readback and a separate
writer activation. A local test helper or an advertised ceiling never substitutes for the retained
artifact's actual behavior. There is no new creator control, production vocabulary or persisted
writer in this preparation.
