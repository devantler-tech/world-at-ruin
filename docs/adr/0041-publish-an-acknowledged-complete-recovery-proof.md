# Publish an acknowledged complete recovery proof

Status: accepted for the default-off experiment in #1327; production adoption remains #1315.

## Context

ADR 0040 produces complete recovery authority only in the live process that
received every barrier ACK and exact readback. A process death can lose that
result. A visible owner row or barrier nonce cannot repair missing acknowledgment.
Persisting diagnostics alone must not manufacture a publishing or allocation handle.

## Decision

The complete opaque recovery result owns one shared, terminal publication attempt.
Copies and independently constructed publisher wrappers share that bit. Consumption
precedes storage I/O, including cancellation and uncertainty. Only the originating
accepted result can construct a publisher; neither decoding nor detached diagnostics
can restore it. Publication offers no allocation, retry, takeover or release API.

One private, system-owned, create-only schema-1 proof arbitrates the entire original
generation, independent of incarnation, owner ID or document version. It binds the
complete original generation/incarnation/root/handoff, namespace/Fleet, membership
and issued inventory, recovery owner ID and its ACK version, and every original
actor/attempt/name/UID/resourceVersion with its outcome and acknowledged barrier
version. Canonical inventory order, exact membership and distinct barrier versions
are required. Empty issued inventories remain valid with explicit empty arrays;
complete, mixed, all-allocated and empty shapes are permanently ledgered. Missing,
substituted or unknown outcomes cannot be complete evidence.

The writer serializes only its opaque result, validates those exact bytes through
the permanent strict decoder, then makes one create-only write with client read and
write permissions zero. One bounded deadline covers write ACK and readback. Exact
collection/key/system owner and a valid ACK version are required. Readback verifies
that exact version, private permissions and decoded complete content, plus the
original private owner, handoff and draining root at independently pinned versions.
Any lost, partial, canceled, conflicting or changed response leaves no opaque
publication result. A later visible row cannot repair it or authorize another write.

Only the live publisher's accepted opaque result exports detached publication pins:
the original full recovery observation and acknowledged proof version. A separate
read-only boundary validates a proof against independently retained pins; it returns
diagnostics and never a publisher, recovery reservation or allocation capability.
The native supervisor retains these pins while the publisher dies. This disposable
channel does not prove authenticated durable production custody or database-rollback
survival, and is not a production quarantine-release mechanism.

## Crash boundaries and evidence

Before submission there may be no proof row; after commit before ACK there may be
a row without acknowledgment; after ACK before exact readback there may be a row
without complete publication acceptance; after exact readback before pin export the
origin process may have accepted publication but the supervisor has no trusted pins.
Every such process is killed and joined. Same-ID and competing fresh processes remain
excluded by the original owner row and cannot reconstruct authority from storage.
Only complete live ACK/readback plus successful independent pin retention permits
diagnostic readback in a fresh process. Cancellation and lost storage replies use
the actual native storage boundary, not a synthetic persistence model.

The native trial joins at least two original frozen targets, actual Nakama/PostgreSQL
proof storage, owned kube-apiserver/etcd barriers, successful and mixed outcomes,
omitted/changed inventory, publisher competition, reply loss and each process-death
seam. Existing unfenced HTTP-200 and fenced HTTP-409 controls remain required.

The new family registers permanent schema history, mixed-outcome goldens, strict
reader assertions and every persisted write site. Normal artifacts and retained
serving readers are unchanged. The native publication entrypoint is disabled unless
explicitly enabled, is tracked by #1316, and runs on both mandatory architectures.
Authenticated durable pin custody, exclusive mutation authority, serving/retained
reader adoption and quarantine release remain undelivered parent #1315/#793 gates.
