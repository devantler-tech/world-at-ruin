# Stature reader evidence

## What the frame proves

`stature-front.png` is a first-party native Godot 4.7.1 Forward+ / Metal frame on Apple M2 Pro. The real Player and CharacterStore reader render three owned fixture documents with the same historical wanderer, shirt, trousers and worn boots: short legs (0.85/0.85), the unchanged recipe, and tall legs (1.2/1.2). All actors retain the same world Y; the tool performs no individual vertical alignment. The reader adjusts only expanded visual roots.

The fixture files are disposable and removed after acceptance. Played character, vault, recovery and update-history files are not used. Reproduce windowed from the repository root with `WAR_STATURE_CAPTURE_DIR=/absolute/owned/output godot --path client --resolution 1280x720 res://tools/stature_capture.tscn`. Headless execution refuses to manufacture frame evidence.

## Independent behavior evidence

The trusted stature scenes precompute all eight historical identities with a frozen, checksum-pinned factory on the same native runtime, then compare candidate parsing and exact fingerprints. They also pin historical visual-root positions, independent knee/ankle length changes, actual skinned body height, triangle clearance at knees and ankles, and refused destructive writes with released locks. A real flat-floor Player probe covers four presets × neutral, short, tall and both mixed leg factors. Every capsule is grounded and keeps its exact shape and offset; body and boot contact stay within 3 mm of the historical control. The observed maximum corrected sole drift is approximately 1.49 mm. The uncorrected reader fails this oracle, moving equal-short soles approximately 12 cm upward and equal-tall soles approximately 16 cm downward.

The separate boot scene consumes the seeded save through the actual game and observes installed requirements at the real UpdateCheck boundary. Historical state remains schema4/capability7; expanded state requires schema5/capability8. Byte preservation and recovery refusal of incompatible targets are asserted.

## Limits and remaining gaps

This is compatibility evidence, not a new race, creation option or AAA activation. Existing shoulder/cloth silhouettes, flat clothing detail, hand pose and approximately 4 cm of historical boot-floor clipping remain visible and unchanged. Knee/ankle clearance is measured relative to historical equipment; the test does not claim universal garment fit or better pre-existing art. Standing body-height checks use a neutral skinned body; the capture’s internal CPU height proxy is not a morph-inclusive measurement. New leg settings remain absent from production creation controls and writer vocabulary. A retained published whole-app reader still needs independent artifact-boot proof before any later writer activation.
